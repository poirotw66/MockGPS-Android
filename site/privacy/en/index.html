BloomWalk GPS Privacy Policy

This is a deployable draft. It is not a live policy URL until the owner publishes it under HTTPS.

BloomWalk GPS is a developer and QA mock-location app. Core mock location, favorites, saved routes, and settings stay on the device until you delete them or uninstall the app.

Services

  • Maps: OpenFreeMap / OpenStreetMap tiles in the app.
  • Online search and road routing: BloomWalk HTTPS gateway and Geoapify, only after an explicit Search or Plan action.
  • Purchases: Google Play Billing for the one-time remove_ads_lifetime product. Purchase tokens are sent to the gateway for verification.
  • Ads and consent: Google AdMob and UMP on the free tier only. We do not send mock or real coordinates, search queries, routes, favorites, or purchase tokens to the ads SDK.
  • Hosting: Cloudflare Workers and Durable Objects for the gateway and quota ledger.

Retention

Device data remains until you delete it. Gateway quota records use a rolling 24-hour window. Purchase grants remain while active. The app does not create a BloomWalk account, so there is no in-app account-deletion control.
